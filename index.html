2. Principle

JustDeckBuilder works without a user account, advertising or analytics/tracking services. Your collection, decks, settings and downloaded rules and images are stored only locally on your device. The developer does not receive any of this data.

3. Connections to third parties

For some features the app connects to the following services. For technical reasons your IP address is transmitted to the respective provider:

  • Scryfall (scryfall.com, USA): card data, online search and card images.
  • Wizards of the Coast (magic.wizards.com): downloading the official Comprehensive Rules – only when you trigger it.
  • Optional, only if you enter an API key yourself: Anthropic, OpenAI, an OpenAI-compatible service you specify, or DeepL. The rule text to be translated and your API key are transmitted when you start a translation.

The privacy policies of these providers apply in addition. Legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR.

4. Files

CSV and deck files are only read or written when you select them yourself via the system file picker.

5. API keys

API keys are stored encrypted (Android Keystore) on the device and only sent to the respective service.

6. Google Play, donations and backup

If you obtain the app via Google Play or make a voluntary donation there, Google's privacy policy applies in addition. Payment data is processed exclusively by Google; the app never receives it. If Android backup is enabled, app data may be backed up according to your Google account settings.

7. Notifications

While an import or downloads run in the background, the app shows a progress notification. No data is transmitted for this.

8. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection (Art. 15–21 GDPR) and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.
